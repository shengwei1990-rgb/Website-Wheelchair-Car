FAQ

Common questions

How far in advance should I book?

Book at least 24 hours ahead, and a few days ahead for airport and Johor trips. We take same-day bookings when the van is free, with a $10 urgent surcharge if it's less than 3 hours' notice.

Will the driver help me board?

Yes. The driver operates the lift, secures the wheelchair with 4-point tie-downs and fastens the seatbelt. We go door-to-door at ground or lift level. We don't carry passengers up or down stairs or provide nursing care.

Can I stay in my motorised wheelchair?

Yes. Most motorised wheelchairs fit on our lift, which takes up to 300 kg for rider and chair together. Tell us the make or width if you're unsure.

Do you provide a wheelchair?

No. Please bring your own. Many hospitals and community centres lend them out.

What do I need for a Johor trip?

A valid passport for each passenger, and the Malaysia Digital Arrival Card for non-Malaysians. Checkpoint traffic can be heavy at peak times, so we'll suggest the best departure time when we confirm your trip.

How do I pay?

PayNow, cash or bank transfer, at the end of the trip. Dialysis packages are paid at the start of each month.

What if my appointment runs late?

Not a problem. Call or WhatsApp us when you're done. Two-way trips include return within 4 hours. Waiting beyond that is $8 per 15 minutes.
